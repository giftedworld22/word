DELSU Extends 2026/2027 Post-UTME Registration Deadline: New Closing Date Announced

Posted on 9th August, 2026.

Delta State University, Abraka (DELSU), has extended the deadline for its 2026/2027 Post-UTME and Direct Entry (DE) screening registration, giving prospective candidates additional time to complete their applications.

The registration deadline has been extended to August 15, 2026, according to current admission information published for the exercise. Candidates who have not completed their applications are advised to use the additional time to finish their registration and ensure that all required information is correctly submitted.

Important date correction: August 15, 2026 falls on a Saturday, not Friday. Therefore, for accuracy, this article refers to the deadline as Saturday, August 15, 2026. Current admission information also gives the closing time as 12:00 midnight.

DELSU Post-UTME Deadline Extended

The extension is important for candidates who were unable to complete the DELSU Post-UTME or Direct Entry screening registration before the earlier deadline.

The 2026/2027 application exercise opened on Friday, May 29, 2026, and the latest information indicates that the portal will remain open until Saturday, August 15, 2026.

This means eligible candidates now have additional time to purchase the form, complete the online application and submit the required information.

The published admission information states that UTME candidates need a minimum score of 150 and that DELSU should be their first-choice institution.

Who Is Eligible for DELSU Post-UTME?

Candidates who selected Delta State University, Abraka, as their first-choice institution in the 2026 UTME and scored at least 150 are eligible to apply, subject to the university's other admission requirements.

Candidates who did not initially choose DELSU may still be able to apply if they change their first-choice institution to DELSU through JAMB before the application deadline.

Prospective applicants should make sure that any change of institution is properly reflected on their JAMB profile before completing their DELSU application.

Direct Entry Candidates Can Also Apply

The screening exercise also covers eligible Direct Entry candidates seeking admission into DELSU undergraduate programmes.

According to the published admission information, Direct Entry applicants may qualify with credentials such as NCE, OND, HND, First Degree or A' Level qualifications, provided the qualification is relevant to the intended programme and meets the university's requirements.

Direct Entry candidates should carefully review the requirements for their chosen course before submitting their applications.

DELSU Post-UTME Application Fee

The application fee listed for the 2026/2027 DELSU Post-UTME exercise is ₦2,000 plus ₦1,000 bank charge.

Applicants should make payments only through the appropriate university application platform and should keep evidence of successful payment and application submission.

The DELSU online portal provides application and payment services for candidates.

How to Apply for DELSU Post-UTME 2026/2027

Eligible candidates can follow these general steps:

  1. Visit the official DELSU admission/application portal.
  2. Select the 2026/2027 Post-UTME application.
  3. Enter your JAMB registration number.
  4. Complete the required personal and academic information.
  5. Pay the prescribed ₦2,000 plus ₦1,000 for bank charge application fee.
  6. Provide your O'Level information and other requested details.
  7. Upload the required passport photograph and documents where applicable.
  8. Carefully review the information entered.
  9. Submit the completed application.
  10. Print or save your application slip for future reference.

The current application information says candidates should print the completed form after submission and keep it safely.

Candidates Should Upload Their O'Level Results

One of the important requirements candidates should pay attention to is the uploading of their O'Level results to the JAMB portal.

Candidates awaiting their O'Level results may be able to apply, but they are expected to upload their results to JAMB once they become available. The published DELSU admission information warns that candidates who fail to upload the required results may not be considered for admission.

Applicants should therefore check their JAMB profile and make sure their results and other admission details are properly updated.

Don't Wait Until the Last Day

Although DELSU has provided candidates with additional time, applicants should avoid waiting until the final hours of the registration period.

Late registration can create problems if a candidate experiences:

  • Internet connection issues
  • Payment difficulties
  • Incorrect information
  • Problems uploading documents
  • JAMB profile issues
  • Difficulty accessing the application portal

Completing the registration early gives candidates time to correct mistakes before the portal closes.

Check DELSU's Official Information Before Applying

Candidates should rely primarily on information released by Delta State University and its official admission platforms when making decisions about their applications.

Final Reminder to DELSU Applicants

The extension of the 2026/2027 DELSU Post-UTME and Direct Entry screening registration deadline to August 15, 2026 gives prospective students another opportunity to complete their applications.

Candidates who meet the requirements should take advantage of the extension and complete their registration before the portal closes.

Remember: The date August 15, 2026 is a Saturday, so candidates should not rely on the description "Friday, August 15." The currently published information gives Saturday, August 15, 2026, at 12:00 midnight as the closing point.
